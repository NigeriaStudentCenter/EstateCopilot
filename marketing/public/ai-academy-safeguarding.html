Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy

Applies to: AI Academy for Teens (learners aged 10–17).
Owner: [Organisation legal name] · Last reviewed: [Month Year] · Next review: [Month Year] (reviewed at least annually).

1. Our commitment

AI Academy is committed to protecting the welfare of every child and young person who takes part in our programmes. The welfare of the child is our paramount concern. We will act in the best interests of the child, take all concerns seriously, and respond to them promptly and appropriately. This policy applies to everyone working for or on behalf of AI Academy — employees, tutors, contractors and volunteers.

2. Who is responsible

Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL): [Name] — [safeguarding@…] · [+234 … / +44 …]

Deputy DSL: [Name] — [email]

The DSL is responsible for handling safeguarding concerns, keeping records, and liaising with parents and, where necessary, the relevant authorities. If you cannot reach the DSL, contact the Deputy DSL.

3. How we work with under-18s

  • We communicate with the parent or guardian, not directly with the child. Enrolment, scheduling, billing and any concern is handled through the parent/guardian. This includes our WhatsApp line and email.
  • Classes are live and online, in a group, on [platform name, e.g. Zoom / Google Meet]. A tutor is never alone one-to-one with a learner. At least [one / two] adult(s) from AI Academy are present in every session.
  • Tutors must not contact learners privately — no private messages, no personal phone numbers, no personal social media, no contact outside scheduled AI Academy sessions and channels.
  • Learners' cameras may be on for engagement; recordings of classes, where made, are for quality and catch-up only, are stored securely, and are not shared outside the class. Parents are told if a session is recorded.
  • Private/direct chat between participants is disabled where the platform allows it; group chat is moderated by the tutor.

4. Safer recruitment & conduct

  • Everyone working with learners provides references and identity verification (NIN/BVN) and agrees to this policy and a code of conduct before starting.
  • Staff and tutors will: treat all young people with respect; maintain professional boundaries; not use language or behaviour that is inappropriate, discriminatory, frightening or humiliating; and report any concern about a colleague's conduct to the DSL.
  • Staff and tutors will not: arrange to meet a learner in person; give or lend money or gifts; take or share photos or video of a learner except through official AI Academy channels for an agreed purpose.

5. What we mean by a safeguarding concern

A concern includes any sign, disclosure or allegation of physical, emotional or sexual abuse, neglect, bullying (including online), grooming, exploitation, or a young person being at risk of harm — whether the source of harm is inside or outside AI Academy.

6. Raising a concern

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services first (Nigeria: 112 or 767; UK: 999), then tell the DSL.

Otherwise, report any worry — however small — to the DSL at [safeguarding@…] or [phone]. You do not need to be certain; it is not your job to investigate. Parents, learners, tutors and members of the public can all raise a concern this way.

What happens next: the DSL acknowledges the concern within one working day, records it, decides on next steps (which may include speaking with the parent/guardian, adjusting arrangements, or referring to the appropriate authority), and keeps the person who raised it informed as far as is appropriate. Concerns about the DSL themselves should go to [named senior person / email].

7. Records & confidentiality

Safeguarding concerns and actions are recorded factually and stored securely, separately from ordinary learner records, and accessed only by those who need to know. Personal data is handled in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (and the UK GDPR for UK-based families). Information is shared with external agencies only where there is a lawful basis and it is necessary to protect a child.

This policy is reviewed at least once a year, and after any significant safeguarding incident or change in the law or in how AI Academy delivers its classes. Questions about this policy: [safeguarding@…].
