Referral Programme

Refer a landlord, earn every month they stay.

If you know landlords — you're an agent, a property manager, a caretaker, or you just have friends with property — you can earn a commission for every one who joins EstateCopilot through your link. The programme runs on Kolo, our partner platform, and it's free to join.

👤 For: Agents, managers, anyone with landlord contacts ⏱️ Read time: About 5 minutes 🎯 Level: Total beginner
How you get paidYou earn a share of the subscription fee for every landlord who signs up with your link — and you keep earning it each month for their first year, not just once. Your exact rate is shown on your dashboard when you join.

1

Create your partner account

It takes about a minute and only needs your phone number.

  1. Open the partner portal

    Go to the Refer & earn link in the EstateCopilot website footer, or open the partner portal link an EstateCopilot team member sent you.

  2. Sign in with your phone number

    Enter your mobile number and the one-time code you receive. There's no password to remember — the code is how you log in each time.

  3. Fill in your name and payout details

    Add your name and, when you're ready to be paid, your bank account under Wallet. You can add the bank details later — you don't need them to start sharing your link.

2

Get your referral link

  1. Open the Links tab

    Your personal referral link is there, ready to copy. It looks like estatecopilot.org/?ref=YOURCODE. Everyone who opens the site through it is tagged to you.

  2. Copy it — or make a link straight to a page

    You can share the plain link, or point it at a specific page (for example the sign-up page) so the landlord lands exactly where you want. Both still carry your code.

Your code sticks for 30 daysIf a landlord clicks your link today and signs up a week later — even after clicking away to pay — the referral is still credited to you.
3

Share it the right way

  1. Send it with a short, honest pitch

    Something like: “EstateCopilot handles tenant checks, rent collection and repairs for Nigerian landlords — here's a link if you want to try it.” Keep it plain; landlords respond to clarity, not hype.

  2. Good places to share

    Your WhatsApp status and landlord groups, a pinned message in a building's tenants/owners chat, your email signature, or one-to-one with clients you already manage property for.

  3. What not to do

    Don't spam groups you're not part of, don't sign people up on their behalf, and don't promise things EstateCopilot doesn't do. Referrals that look like abuse can be reversed.

4

Track signups & get paid

  1. Home shows clicks and signups

    You'll see how many people opened your link and how many became paying landlords.

  2. Wallet shows what you've earned

    Each confirmed landlord shows as a commission. Commissions become payable once the landlord's subscription payment clears.

  3. Add your bank account to be paid

    Under Wallet, add your account number and bank. Payouts are sent on a regular cycle to the verified account — you don't need to request each one.

A 5% tax is withheldAs required in Nigeria, a small withholding tax is deducted from each payout and remitted on your behalf. Your Wallet shows the gross amount, the deduction, and what lands in your account.
