Guides for landlords & tenants

Everything you need to run your properties — or live in one — on EstateCopilot. Pick a guide below.

For landlords The Landlord Guide Signing up, touring your dashboard, messaging tenants on WhatsApp, listing properties, and collecting rent — written so anyone can follow it, step by step. For tenants The Tenant Guide Accepting your landlord's invitation, finding your way around your tenant portal, messaging your landlord, and paying rent. For landlords & tenants Vacancies & Repair Requests Who does what, and how: listing a vacant unit for viewings, and reporting or dispatching a repair from first message to paid artisan.
Can't find what you need? Landlords can reach support from Settings in the portal. Tenants can message their landlord directly from the Correspondence tab.
