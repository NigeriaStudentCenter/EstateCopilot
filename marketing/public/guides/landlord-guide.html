Landlord Guide

Welcome to EstateCopilot — let's get your properties online.

This guide walks you through everything, one small step at a time. You don't need to know anything about computers or property software already — if you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.

👤 For: Landlords & property managers ⏱️ Read time: About 15 minutes 🎯 Level: Total beginner

Signing up & getting approved

First, let's create your account. It takes about five minutes, and you'll pay a single flat fee — no surprise charges later.

  1. Go to the sign-up page

    Open estatecopilot.org in your phone or computer's browser, then tap the green List your property button at the top of the page.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The EstateCopilot homepage with the green "List your property" button in the top-right corner highlighted with an arrow]

  2. Fill in your details

    You'll be asked for:

    • Your full name
    • Your email address
    • Your phone number — use your WhatsApp number, since this is how tenants and your copilot will reach you
    • A password (at least 8 characters — mix letters and numbers so it's hard to guess)
    • Your state — the Nigerian state where most of your properties are

    When everything looks right, tap Continue to payment.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The sign-up form with each field labelled — Full name, Email, Phone (WhatsApp), Password, State — and the "Continue to payment" button highlighted]
    TipUse a phone number you check every day — reminders, tenant messages, and approvals all come through it.

  3. Pay the monthly fee

    EstateCopilot costs a flat ₦10,000 a month — no matter how many properties you list. You'll be sent to a secure Paystack page to pay. This is the same trusted payment page used by banks and big shops in Nigeria, so it's safe.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The "Pay ₦10,000/month on Paystack" screen, with the Paystack logo visible for reassurance]

  4. Wait for your account to be switched on

    Once your payment clears, our team turns your account on — this usually happens within a few hours. You'll know it's ready when you can log in without seeing a "payment pending" message.

    Why the wait?During the pilot, a real person double-checks every new landlord before switching on full access — just an extra layer of care while we're getting started together.

  5. Log in for the first time

    Go to landlord.estatecopilot.org, type in the email and password you chose, and tap Log in. You'll land straight on your Dashboard.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Landlord Login screen with the Email, Password fields and "Log in" button highlighted]

3

WhatsApp & messaging your tenants

This is the heart of EstateCopilot. Your tenants can message you the same way they message anyone else — and your copilot helps you reply, without ever sending anything on your behalf without your OK.

How it actually worksA tenant sends a message → it shows up on your Tenancies page instantly → your copilot writes a suggested reply → the reply waits for you in your AI Inbox → nothing is ever sent until you approve it.

  1. Open your AI Inbox

    Click AI Inbox in the sidebar. You'll see a list of drafted replies, one for each tenant message that's come in.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The AI Inbox list view, showing 2–3 pending draft replies with tenant name and a short preview of each message]

  2. Read the tenant's message and the suggested reply

    Open a draft to see exactly what the tenant said, and the reply your copilot has written for them in your voice. Read it carefully — you know your tenants and your properties best.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: An open draft showing the tenant's original message on the left and the AI-suggested reply on the right, with Approve, Edit and Reject buttons visible]

  3. Approve, edit, or reject the reply

    • Approve — sends the reply exactly as written
    • Edit — change a few words first, then send
    • Reject — don't send it; you'll reply another way (e.g. a phone call)
    ImportantNothing is ever sent to a tenant automatically. Every single message waits for your decision first — that's the whole point of the AI Inbox.

  4. Send a rent reminder yourself, any time

    You don't have to wait for a tenant to write first. Open Tenancies, pick a tenant from the list, and tap Send rent reminder — it goes out over WhatsApp right away, and gets logged in that tenant's message history.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Tenancies page with a tenant selected and the green "Send rent reminder" button highlighted]

  5. Read the whole conversation history

    Every message — WhatsApp, email, and reminders — is saved under Correspondence on that tenant's page, in order, so you never lose track of what was said.

4

Adding & managing your properties

Let's get your first property onto the platform.

  1. Add a new property

    Click Properties, then Add property. Fill in:

    • Title — e.g. "2-Bedroom Flat, Ikeja"
    • Address and LGA (Local Government Area)
    • Type — long-term rental or short-let
    • Rent amount and caution deposit
    • Tenement/Municipal ID (optional, for tracking local levies)
    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The "Add property" form with every field labelled, and the "Save" button highlighted]

  2. Add photos and a short description

    Good photos help a lot if you plan to advertise this unit. Paste in image links, and write a short, honest description of the property.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The property edit screen showing the photo gallery section and description text box]

  3. Decide whether to advertise it publicly

    Got a vacant unit? Flip on Advertise on public site and it appears on our public listings page, where anyone can request a viewing. This is covered in full in the Vacancies & Repair Requests guide.

  4. Invite a tenant

    Already have a tenant moving in? Scroll to Invite a tenant on that property's card, enter their name and WhatsApp number, and tap Generate link. Copy the link it gives you and send it to your tenant — this is how they create their own account.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The "Invite a tenant" panel with Tenant name, Tenant phone fields, the generated invite link, and Copy link + Share Tenant Guide buttons]
    TipSend the invite link and the Tenant Guide together — your tenant will know exactly what to do next, without having to ask you.

  5. Connect your bank account

    So that rent lands directly in your bank account — not ours — go to Settings and enter your account number and bank. We verify it instantly and every rent payment from then on pays you directly.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Settings page's bank details section with account number field, bank dropdown, and the verified account name shown once confirmed]
5

Getting paid & day-to-day work

The last piece — how money moves, and how you keep on top of the small jobs every week.

  1. Set up how rent gets paid

    On a tenant's Tenancies page, choose Pay in full or Installments, then tap Generate Paystack payment link(s). Your tenant gets a link (or links) to pay from — every one lands straight in your account.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Rent payment plan section showing Full vs Installments options and a generated installment table with payment links]

  2. Check what's been paid

    Go to Finance & Levies to see every confirmed payment, who it came from, and which property it's for — always up to date.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Finance & Levies page's "Rent received" list showing several confirmed payments]

  3. Keep local levies up to date

    Also in Finance & Levies, log each property's Tenement Rate, LAWMA and similar dues, and mark them cleared once paid. A caution deposit can't be released to a tenant while a levy on that property is still in arrears — this protects you automatically.

  4. Handle repairs

    When a tenant reports something broken, it appears in Maintenance. Full details — including sending it out to an artisan — are in the Vacancies & Repair Requests guide.

  5. Confirm viewing & visit requests

    Bookings lists every property-viewing and repair-visit request, grouped by date like a calendar. Tap Confirm or Cancel on each one.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Bookings page grouped by date, with Confirm and Cancel buttons on a pending viewing request]

  6. Ask for legal help when you need it

    Something outside the everyday — a difficult tenant, a notice-to-quit? Go to Legal and describe the situation. A real lawyer can quote you for the work.

You've got thisThat's the whole platform. Most landlords only touch three things every week: the AI Inbox, Finance & Levies, and Maintenance. Everything else runs quietly in the background.
