Artisan Network

Get found by landlords. Quote on real jobs.

EstateCopilot connects verified tradespeople — electricians, plumbers, tilers, AC and generator technicians, carpenters and more — with landlords and tenants who need work done. This guide shows you how to set up your profile, get verified, and start getting work.

👤 For: Artisans & tradespeople ⏱️ Read time: About 6 minutes 🎯 Level: Total beginner
Two ways you get work 1. The directory. Landlords and tenants search verified artisans by trade and area, see your track record, and call you or send a quote request.
2. The jobs board. Landlords post repair jobs; you send a price, or book a visit first if you need to see it.

1

Create your account

  1. Open the artisan portal

    Go to artisans.estatecopilot.org on your phone — or tap Join as an artisan in the EstateCopilot website footer.

  2. Sign in with your phone number

    Enter your mobile number and the one-time code you receive by SMS. That code is how you log in every time — there's no password.

  3. Add your name and base location

    First time in, you'll set your name, your state, and the LGA you're based in. You can add a business name and a short bio from your profile afterwards.

2

Your trades & coverage area

  1. Pick your trades

    Choose from the trade list (Electrician, Plumber, Tiler, AC Technician, Generator Technician, Carpenter, Painter, Welder, Borehole, POP & Ceiling, and more — up to six). Set years of experience for each and mark one as your primary trade.

  2. Set the areas you cover

    Your base LGA is always included. Add the other LGAs you're willing to travel to — jobs and quote requests are matched to artisans who cover that area, so wider coverage means more work.

3

Get verified — this is what lists you

You won't appear in the public directory until you've passed at least an ID check. It's quick.

  1. Verify your identity with NIN or BVN

    On your profile, enter your NIN or BVN. It's checked against the national database and used only to confirm your name — the number itself is never stored or shown to anyone.

  2. You're now listed

    A successful check moves you to ID-verified and switches your profile live in the directory. Higher tiers — reference-checked, then EstateCopilot-certified — come later and make you stand out further.

Your scoreEach listed artisan has a 0–100 score built from ratings, jobs completed, verification level and how recently you've been active. A complete profile and steady activity move it up.
4

Work photos & availability

  1. Add photos of finished work

    Upload clear photos of jobs you've done — a neat DB board, a tiled bathroom, a fitted wardrobe. The first photo becomes your directory cover. This is the single biggest thing that wins a call.

  2. Keep your availability honest

    Set Open, Busy or Away. Landlords can see it, so keeping it current means you get contacted when you can actually take the work.

5

Winning jobs

  1. Direct requests

    When someone picks you from the directory, their request appears in your portal under Direct requests, with their phone number. Call them back quickly — speed wins these.

  2. Open jobs on the board

    The Jobs tab lists repair jobs in your coverage area. Send a price if you're confident from the description, or ask to visit first if you need to see it to quote accurately.

  3. Do good work, get rated

    Finished jobs earn ratings from the landlord or tenant. Those ratings feed your score, which decides how high you appear the next time someone searches your trade.

Direct hires are between you and the clientWhen a landlord or tenant contacts you from the directory, you agree the price and payment directly. EstateCopilot doesn't take a cut of a direct hire.
