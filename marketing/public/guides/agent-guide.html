For estate agents

Verified listings. Real leads. Paid on time.

EstateCopilot alerts you the moment a verified landlord lists a property in your area. Share it with your clients using your own link, turn their viewings into deals, and collect your agent fee through EstateCopilot — your share goes straight to your bank. This guide walks you through it.

👤 For: Estate agents & property marketers ⏱️ Read time: About 5 minutes 💸 Cost: Free to join
How you earn Every listing shows the agent fee the landlord has agreed — usually 10% of the annual rent, paid by the tenant. The tenant pays it to EstateCopilot through Paystack and it's split on the spot: 90% to your bank, 10% to EstateCopilot. No chasing, no cash.

1

Register

  1. Open the agents page

    Go to estatecopilot.org/agents and tap Join free as an agent.

  2. Fill in your details

    Your name, email, WhatsApp number and a password. Add your agency name and ESVARBON / NIESV number if you have them — landlords see who you are when you bring them a tenant.

  3. Pick the states you work in

    Tick every state you cover. You'll be alerted to new listings in those states only, and you can change them any time under Payouts & profile.

2

Add your bank so you get paid

Do this firstYou can't record a deal until your bank is added — that's what lets Paystack pay your share to you automatically.

  1. Open Payouts & profile

    In your dashboard, open the Payouts & profile tab, choose your bank and enter your 10-digit account number.

  2. We check it with your bank

    The account name is confirmed with your bank before it's saved, so money can't go to the wrong person.

3

Share listings with your clients

  1. Watch your alerts

    When a property is listed in your states it appears in Alerts and Listings (and in your email), with the rent and your agent fee.

  2. Share your personal link

    Every listing has Share on WhatsApp and Copy link. The link carries your code, so clients who open it see “Shared with you by [your name]” and any viewing they book comes to you.

4

Turn viewings into deals

  1. Contact your lead straight away

    A viewing request through your link lands in Leads with the client's name, phone and preferred time — call or WhatsApp them in one tap and mark the viewing arranged.

  2. Record the deal

    When your client agrees to rent, tap Record deal. Their details are filled in from the lead; check the agreed rent and send it.

  3. The landlord confirms

    The landlord gets a one-click link to confirm (or dispute) the deal. As soon as they confirm, the tenant is emailed a secure link to pay the agent fee — and you get the link too, to send on WhatsApp.

5

Getting paid

  1. The tenant pays through Paystack

    Card, bank transfer or USSD. Paystack splits it immediately — your share settles to your bank, usually the next working day.

  2. Track it in Deals

    Each deal shows its status: Waiting for landlord → Waiting for tenant payment → Paid, with exactly what you receive. Your total earnings are at the top of your dashboard.

ExampleA ₦4,000,000/year flat with a 10% agent fee: the tenant pays ₦400,000, EstateCopilot keeps ₦40,000, and ₦360,000 goes to you.
