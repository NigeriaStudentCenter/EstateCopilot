Tenant Guide

Welcome — here's everything you need as a tenant.

Your landlord uses EstateCopilot to manage your tenancy. This short guide shows you how to set up your own account, talk to your landlord, and pay your rent — all from your phone.

👤 For: Tenants ⏱️ Read time: About 8 minutes 🎯 Level: Total beginner
Given to you by your landlordThis guide is shared automatically whenever a landlord invites a tenant onto EstateCopilot — if you're reading this, your landlord has already added your unit to the platform and sent you an invite link.

  2. Part 2 — A tour of your tenant portal

1

Accepting your invitation

Your landlord sent you a link — usually over WhatsApp or SMS. Let's use it to create your account.

  1. Open the invite link your landlord sent you

    It looks something like tenant.estatecopilot.org/?code=xxxxxx. Tap it, or paste it into your phone's browser.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: A WhatsApp chat bubble from the landlord containing the invite link, with a finger tapping it]

  2. Make sure you're on the "Create account" tab

    The page opens with two tabs at the top: Create account and Log in. Since this is your first time, stay on Create account. Your Tenancy ID / invite code box will already be filled in for you.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The tenant sign-up screen with "Create account" tab active, and the Tenancy ID / invite code field shown already filled in and greyed out]

  3. Fill in your name, email and a password

    • Full name — your real name, as it appears on your tenancy agreement
    • Email — an address you check
    • Password — at least 8 characters

    Tap Create account to finish.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The completed sign-up form with Full name, Email and Password filled in, and the green "Create account" button highlighted]
    TipLost the link, or it stopped working? Ask your landlord to send it again from their Properties page — the same button that made it the first time.

  4. Log in next time

    You only need the invite link once. After that, just go to tenant.estatecopilot.org, tap the Log in tab, and enter your email and password.

2

A tour of your tenant portal

Across the top of every page you'll see four tabs. Here's what each one does.

  1. Overview — your tenancy at a glance

    This is your home page. It shows your lease status, when it renews, your yearly rent, your electricity balance, and — if a payment is due — a reminder with a button to pay it.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The tenant Overview page showing the Lease status, Verification, Annual rent and Electricity balance cards, with a "Next payment due" banner]

  2. Correspondence — your messages

    Every conversation with your landlord — including WhatsApp — lives here, oldest at the top.

  3. Maintenance — report a repair

    Something broken? This is where you tell your landlord about it.

  4. Agreement — your tenancy agreement

    Read and sign the official agreement for your tenancy here, once your landlord sends it.

  5. Logging out

    Tap Log out at the top-right any time — handy if you're on a shared phone or computer.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The top navigation bar with all four tabs — Overview, Correspondence, Maintenance, Agreement — and the "Log out" link, each numbered]
3

Talking to your landlord (including WhatsApp)

You never have to guess how to reach your landlord — one place holds every conversation, no matter which way it happened.

  1. Open Correspondence

    Tap the Correspondence tab to see your full message history with your landlord — this includes WhatsApp reminders they've sent you, and any notes they've logged.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Correspondence page showing a chat-style thread with the landlord's messages on the left and the tenant's on the right, labelled with channel — e.g. "WhatsApp" or "Portal message"]

  2. Send a message

    Type into the box at the bottom that says "Type a message to your landlord…" and tap Send.

    Good to knowYour landlord's replies are reviewed by them before they're sent to you — so a reply may take a little while to arrive. It's still your landlord's own words, just double-checked first.

  3. WhatsApp reminders

    Sometimes your landlord will send you a rent reminder straight to your WhatsApp number. You don't need to do anything differently — it also shows up automatically in your Correspondence tab, so you always have both copies.

4

Paying rent & viewing documents

Two more things every tenant needs: a simple way to pay, and somewhere to keep your paperwork.

  1. Check what you owe

    On your Overview page, look for the amber Next payment due box. It shows the amount and the date it's due.

  2. Pay with one tap

    Tap Pay now via Paystack. You'll be taken to a secure Paystack payment page — pay by card or bank transfer, the same way you'd pay for anything else online. The payment goes straight to your landlord.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The "Next payment due" banner with amount and due date, and the "Pay now via Paystack" button highlighted]
    Never pay outside the appIf anyone asks you to send rent by a method other than the Paystack link in your portal, check with your landlord directly before paying anything.

  3. Read your tenancy agreement

    Once your landlord sends it, open the Agreement tab and read it fully.

  4. Sign it

    At the bottom of the agreement, type your full legal name, tick "I have read and agree to the terms of this tenancy agreement", then tap Sign agreement. Your signature and the date are saved automatically.

    Image placeholder[IMAGE: The Agreement page's signing form with the full-name field, the confirmation checkbox ticked, and the "Sign agreement" button highlighted]

  5. Have a repair? See the next guide

    Reporting a broken appliance or a leak works the same simple way — it's covered fully in Vacancies & Repair Requests.

That's itFour tabs, one login. Everything about your tenancy lives in one place, and your landlord sees exactly the same information you do — no surprises on either side.
