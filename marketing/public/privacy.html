Who we are

We are the "data controller" for the personal data described below — we decide what it's used for and how.

This one policy also covers the shared WhatsApp line that fields enquiries for two related campaigns — AI Academy (practical AI/technology classes) and Becoming a New Nigerian (a mindset and nation-building community under the Return to Nigeria Initiative) — because messaging any of the three starts on the same number. Each has its own scope, described in section 3.

2

What we collect, and why

Landlords

Name, email, phone number, a password (stored hashed, never in plain text), state, subscription payment records, and the connected bank account you provide so rent can be paid straight to you. We use this to run your account, process your subscription payment, and give you the dashboard (properties, tenancies, finance & levies, maintenance, bookings, legal requests, AI-drafted tenant replies).

Tenants

Name and phone number (added by your landlord when they invite you), the tenancy and rent-payment details needed to run your tenancy, messages you exchange with your landlord (Correspondence), maintenance reports, and your e-signature on the tenancy agreement. Where a landlord runs identity vetting on a prospective tenant, we process the NIN or BVN only to confirm a name match against the national database — the number itself is never stored or shown to anyone, only the match outcome and a display-safe resolved name.

Artisans

Phone number, name, business name and bio, the state/LGA you're based in and willing to cover, your trades and years of experience, work-sample photos, and availability. To verify your identity (which is what lists you publicly), we process your NIN or BVN the same way as above — checked only for a name match, the number itself never stored or shown. Once listed, your name, business name, trades, ratings, work photos and phone number are shown publicly in the artisan directory so landlords and tenants can find and contact you directly — that visibility is the point of joining the directory.

Referral / partner programme (Kolo)

Your phone number, name and bank details (once you add them to be paid), and records of which landlords signed up through your referral link, so we can calculate and pay your commission.

Website & marketing-lead visitors

If you fill in a form on this site — booking a property viewing, requesting an artisan quote, submitting a legal or handyman enquiry — we collect what that form asks for (typically name, phone, and email) to act on your request and, only if you tick the box, to send you WhatsApp updates.

Payments

Rent, subscription and other payments on EstateCopilot are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card number, CVV or full bank login — Paystack handles that directly and we only receive a payment reference and amount.

3

Messaging us on WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp — about EstateCopilot, AI Academy, or Becoming a New Nigerian — or agree to receive WhatsApp messages from us, we process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, and the content of the messages you exchange with us (text, and any images or files you send).
  • Records of consent — when and how you opted in to marketing messages, and any opt-out.
  • Conversation metadata — timestamps, delivery/read status, which of the three services the conversation relates to, and whether it was handled by our automated assistant or a person.

Why. To answer your enquiry; to help you search property listings, contact verified artisans, book viewings, enquire about AI Academy classes, or register for Becoming a New Nigerian; to capture and follow up leads; and, where you've opted in, to send you relevant updates. Our legal basis is your consent for marketing messages, and our legitimate interest — or steps to enter a contract at your request — for responding to enquiries.

Reply STOP any time to unsubscribe from marketing messages on WhatsApp immediately. You can still message us afterwards if you need help — that's not affected.

See section 4 for how the automated assistant works, section 10 for the AI Academy for Teens safeguard, and section 6 for who we share this with.

4

Automated replies & AI

Two features use an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to help draft or generate replies:

  • WhatsApp assistant — may reply to your message automatically. It cannot agree a price, confirm a booking, take payment, or give legal or financial advice — anything like that is flagged for a person.
  • Landlord AI Inbox — drafts a reply to a tenant's message in the landlord's own voice. Nothing is ever sent to a tenant until the landlord reviews and approves it.

Neither makes a decision with a legal or similarly significant effect about you. The message text involved is sent to Anthropic for processing — see section 6.

5

Cookies & local storage

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The only thing we store in your browser is a small marker — for up to 30 days — recording which referral partner's link you arrived from, if any, so that partner is credited correctly if you later sign up as a landlord. It contains no personal data and is never read by anyone but this site.

International transfers

Data is processed primarily in Nigeria and Western Europe (Microsoft Azure West Europe), with some providers based in the United States (Anthropic, Meta, Twilio, Resend) and India (Microsoft support). Where data leaves Nigeria, we rely on the provider's own standard safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as applicable.

8

How long we keep it

We keep account and tenancy records for as long as the account is active, plus a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, accounting or dispute-resolution needs. WhatsApp conversation records are kept for up to 24 months after your last message, then deleted or anonymised, unless we need to keep them longer for a legal obligation. NIN/BVN numbers themselves are never stored at all — see section 2.

Your rights & choices

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete data we don't need to keep for a legal reason.
  • Objection / opt-out — reply STOP to any WhatsApp marketing message, or contact us, at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in section 14. This processing is carried out in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where applicable, UK GDPR.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Access to production systems and the WhatsApp conversation console is restricted to authorised team members. NIN/BVN numbers are never stored — only the outcome of a single verification check. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect what we hold.

12

Requests from public authorities

Any request from a government body, court, regulator or law-enforcement agency for personal data we hold is routed to a named person on our team and is not actioned by anyone else. Before any disclosure we:

  1. Review legal validity — confirm the request is lawful, properly served, within the requesting body's jurisdiction and authority, and specific rather than a blanket demand.
  2. Challenge where appropriate — refuse or formally challenge a request that is unlawful, overbroad, defective, or lacks a valid legal basis, taking legal advice where needed.
  3. Minimise — disclose only the specific data strictly necessary to comply, never a broader export.
  4. Document — record the request, the requesting body, the legal basis relied on, our reasoning, who was involved, what was disclosed, and when, and retain that record.

Where lawful, we notify the affected user before disclosure. We have not provided personal data to a public authority for a national-security request in the past 12 months.
