## 4. Government data-request policy

Adopt this as an internal policy document, then you can truthfully tick all
four boxes on the Meta form ("required legality review", "provisions for
challenging", "data minimization", "documentation").

> **Requests from public authorities for user data.**
> Any request from a government body, court, regulator, or law-enforcement
> agency for personal data we hold is routed immediately to
> [named person / role] and is not actioned by anyone else.
> Before any disclosure we:
> 1. **Review legal validity** — confirm the request is lawful, properly
>    served, within the requesting body's jurisdiction and authority, and
>    specific rather than a blanket demand.
> 2. **Challenge where appropriate** — refuse or formally challenge a
>    request that is unlawful, overbroad, defective, or lacks a valid legal
>    basis, taking legal advice where needed.
> 3. **Minimise** — disclose only the specific data strictly necessary to
>    comply, never a broader export.
> 4. **Document** — record the request, the requesting body, the legal
>    basis relied on, our internal reasoning, who was involved, what was
>    disclosed, and when, and retain that record.
> Where lawful, we notify the affected user before disclosure.

## 5. Privacy policy — WhatsApp section

Insert into the published privacy policy (or a dedicated `/privacy/whatsapp`
page linked from it):

> ### Messaging us on WhatsApp
>
> When you contact EstateCopilot or AI Academy on WhatsApp, or agree to
> receive WhatsApp messages from us, we process:
>
> - **Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name**, and the **content of
>   the messages** you exchange with us (text, and any images or files you
>   send).
> - **Records of consent** — when and how you opted in to marketing
>   messages, and any opt-out.
> - **Conversation metadata** — timestamps, delivery/read status, which
>   brand (EstateCopilot or AI Academy) the conversation relates to, and
>   whether it was handled by our automated assistant or a person.
>
> **Why.** To answer your enquiry; to help you search property listings,

> classes; to capture and follow up leads; and, where you have opted in, to
> send you relevant updates and offers. Our legal basis is your consent for

>
> **Automated assistant.** Replies may be generated by an AI assistant. It
> does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about

> **Children.** AI Academy enrolment conversations on WhatsApp are conducted

> **Who we share it with.** Our service providers acting on our
> instructions: Microsoft (hosting) and Anthropic (AI reply generation).

> (Meta) under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
>
> **International transfers.** Message data is processed in the United
> Kingdom, the European Economic Area, the United States and India. Where
> data leaves the UK/EEA we rely on [Standard Contractual Clauses / UK
> IDTA / adequacy], as applicable.
>
> **Retention.** WhatsApp conversation records are kept for [e.g. 24 months]
> after your last message, then deleted or anonymised, unless we need to
> keep them longer to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.
>
> **Your choices.** Reply **STOP** to opt out of marketing messages at any
> time. To request access to or deletion of your WhatsApp data, contact
> [privacy@estatecopilot.org]. This processing is carried out in line with
> the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the UK GDPR.

## 6. Consent capture (for the marketing site forms)

Marketing (business-initiated / campaign) messages need a logged opt-in.
Add an **unticked** checkbox to the EstateCopilot and AI Academy lead forms:

> ☐ Send me updates and offers from [EstateCopilot / AI Academy] on
> WhatsApp. I can reply STOP at any time. See the Privacy Policy.

Store, per submission: phone, `marketingOptIn`, `brand`, `source`, the exact
checkbox wording (`optInText`), IP, and timestamp — the `WaConsent` model.

Clicking a "chat on WhatsApp" link is **not** marketing consent — it only
permits replying within that conversation.
