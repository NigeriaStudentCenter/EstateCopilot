Scholarship Finder

A guided starting point for funding your studies — in Nigeria or abroad. Tell it your study stage, where you want to study and your field, and it maps out the categories of scholarships that fit, the named programmes to search for, an eligibility check, and a clear application plan with a document checklist. It is a research aid: every detail must be confirmed on the official website.
