Five things this diagnostic just taught you

  • AI predicts text — it doesn’t “know” facts, so it can be confidently wrong. Always ask for a source.
  • Good prompts have a shape — Role, Task, Context, limits. Vague in, vague out.
  • Verify everything — nothing from an AI goes in your work without a second source you found yourself.
  • You can build without code — a clear spec plus no-code tools beats a big vague idea.
  • Use it honestly — add at least 40% of your own thinking, and tell your teacher how AI helped.
